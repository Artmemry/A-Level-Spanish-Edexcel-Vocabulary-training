How this site works, and how your teacher sees your progress

Welcome to El Léxico: A-Level Spanish vocabulary at BBA

For students, parents, carers and staff.

How the site works

These are the word lists you use in class, unit by unit and lesson by lesson. Read a list, or practise it by typing the answers, from Spanish into English and from English into Spanish. Words you get wrong come back sooner, and words you know come back less often, so your time goes on what you have not learnt yet. Review brings back the words that are due today; Self-check tests you on the units you choose, with no corrections until the end.

There is nothing to install and no login: no account, no cookies and no tracking. Nothing leaves your browser until you choose to send it.

Keeping your progress safe

Your progress is saved in the browser you are using, so it is easiest to work on the same device and browser each time. If you open the site on another computer, in another browser or in a private window, your earlier work will not be there. The same happens after your browsing history is cleared. Anything you have already sent to your teacher is safe either way, because your teacher keeps a copy. To take your progress with you, press Back up in Progress. This saves a small file you can keep on your hard drive or OneDrive or email to yourself. On the new device, press Restore and choose that file.

Sending your progress to your teacher

At the end of every activity, press Send to my teacher. You can also send at any time from Progress, or with Send now in the strip at the top of every tab. A Microsoft Form opens with your name and code filled in: press Submit. (If the code box is empty, your code has already been copied for you: paste it in.)

For your name, use your first name and the initial of your surname, e.g. Sophie K.

If the form will not open, press Copy the code in Progress and send it to your teacher by email or Teams. The strip at the top of every tab tells you whether your teacher has received your latest work.

What your teacher sees

Only what is in your code: your name, and your word statistics (words seen, words mastered, accuracy, homework lists done and self-check results). Teachers collect the codes from the form and read them on the department's dashboard.

Seeing your own progress

The Progress tab shows:

  • how many words you have seen;
  • how many you have mastered, meaning remembered over three weeks or more;
  • how many reviews are due today;
  • your accuracy in each direction, unit by unit;
  • which lessons need more work.

Sister site

Exam practice → at the top takes you to A-Level-Spanish-BBA, with the themes, set works, translation and grammar. A homework link from your teacher may take you through both sites in order, with a Next button.
